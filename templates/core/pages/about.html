What we do differently

  • Honest ETAs. Delivery estimates come from real prep times and real distances — not a marketing number.
  • Transparent pricing. Item totals, delivery fee, GST and coupon discounts are itemised on every bill and invoice.
  • Reviews you can trust. Only customers with a delivered order can review a dish, so ratings reflect actual meals.
  • Fair to partners. Restaurant owners get their own panel with live orders, dish controls and earnings.

How it works

  1. Browse the menu or search for a craving.
  2. Add dishes to your cart — mix and match across restaurants.
  3. Check out with a saved address and Cash on Delivery.
  4. Track the order live until it reaches your door.
