Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

  • Account data: name, email, phone number and password (hashed).
  • Order data: addresses, order history and delivery instructions.
  • Location (optional): a coarse delivery location you explicitly share to see nearby restaurants. We never track you in the background.
  • Usage data: pages visited and search queries, to improve the product.

How we use it

To process orders, deliver food, personalise suggestions, prevent fraud, and send transactional emails (order confirmations and status updates). We do not sell your personal data — ever.

Cookies

We use essential cookies for your session, cart and CSRF protection. Analytics cookies are only loaded if enabled and are IP-anonymised.

Sharing

Your delivery details are shared with the restaurant and delivery partner handling your order — only what they need to complete it.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, correct it, or ask us to delete your account and associated data. Email hello@quickbite.com and we'll act within 30 days.

Security

Passwords are stored hashed, connections are protected by HTTPS in production, and access to personal data is limited to staff who need it.
