Cancellation before preparation

Cancel free of charge any time while your order is Pending or Confirmed. Cash-on-Delivery orders need no refund — nothing was paid.

Cancelled or failed orders (prepaid)

If an order paid online is cancelled by you before preparation, by the restaurant, or by us, the full amount is refunded to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.

Wrong, missing or poor-quality items

Report the issue from the order page within 24 hours of delivery with a photo where possible. After verification we refund the affected items — fully or partially depending on the issue.

Non-refundable situations

  • Orders cancelled after the restaurant starts preparing.
  • Complaints raised more than 24 hours after delivery.
  • Change-of-mind requests once food is delivered and acceptable.

Coupons

If an order using a coupon is refunded, the coupon is restored to your account when it is still within its validity window; expired coupons cannot be restored.
